Accepted: .eml, .mbox, .txt — 10 MB per file, 50 MB and 100 files per submission. Submit a whole conversation together: behavioural, thread and stylometric analysis compare each message against the rest of the case.

Evidence handling. Analysis runs entirely on this machine. Accepted messages are preserved unmodified as case evidence, stored under their SHA-256 hash and re-verified on every read. Evidence is never executed, attachments are never extracted to separate files, and URLs found in evidence are never contacted.
