Upload Evidence & Case Information

Accepted: .eml, .mbox, .txt — 10 MB per file, 100 files per submission. Upload a whole conversation together: thread, behavioural and stylometric analysis compare each message against the rest of the set.

Handling. Analysis runs entirely on this machine. Accepted messages are preserved unmodified as case evidence, stored under their SHA-256 hash and re-verified on every read. Evidence is never executed, and attachments are never extracted to separate files. URLs found in evidence are analysed statically and are never contacted.

  • Look-alike domain analysis

  • Communication graph

  • Potential ATT&CK mapping
  • Evidence integrity (SHA-256)
  • Chain of custody
  • Tamper-evident audit log
Findings separate observed evidence from inference and recommendation. The tool reports technical indicators only; it does not determine that a criminal offence occurred.
