How an assessment is produced

  1. Acquisition. Each message is hashed (SHA-256) over its raw bytes before parsing, recorded in the chain of custody, and preserved in the case under that hash.
  2. Analysis. Rules examine sender identity, domains (including Unicode confusables), SPF/DKIM/DMARC, sending behaviour, urgency/authority/secrecy language, financial intent, thread headers, writing style, URLs and attachments.
  3. Fusion. Each finding contributes weight × confidence; categories are capped; corroborating combinations add a documented bonus; the total is normalised to 0–100.
  4. Report. Every finding shows the observed evidence, the inference drawn, its risk contribution and a recommendation. The audit trail is hash-chained.
